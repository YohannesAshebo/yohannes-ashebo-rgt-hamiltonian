# Contributing

Contributions to this research reference are welcome under Apache 2.0.
Please submit only material you are authorized to contribute under that
license, and preserve notices for any third-party material.

For a numerical change, provide the command, input state or preparation,
parameters, software versions and resulting diagnostics. State whether the
change affects the Hamiltonian, the dynamics, a numerical approximation,
units, calibration, or a reporting convention.

Retain negative findings and failed gates. Do not remove or relax a check
solely to obtain a passing result. Distinguish a finite-difference probe from
a full gradient check and a numerical identity from an experimental test.

For a convergence result, include matched continuum inputs, temporal-error
checks and at least three grids when estimating an unknown empirical order.
An interpolated checkpoint is not a separately evolved refined solution.

For a publication, cite the exact release and identify modified equations or
parameters. Citation is a scholarly request, not an extra license condition.
